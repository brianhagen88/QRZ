WHY AMATEUR RADIO?
For me, amateur radio is a mix of communication, experimentation, problem solving, building, learning, and public service.

One day it is HF DX.

The next it is CW, satellites, antennas, emergency communications, or trying to figure out why something that modeled perfectly has a 3:1 SWR.

Same waves. A brighter world.
